About the Clan

Clan creation date: Friday, 7 August 2026

The clan was created by Boss_Hoss and Tito on Friday, 7 August 2026. Both of them played together in a clan named Clan for Wars in late 2025 and early 2026. The clan was discontinued in July 2026, as its leader and co-leaders became busy with university studies and preparation for competitive examinations.

This resulted in the other members of Clan for Wars having to find new clans. After some gradual hopping between clans, Tito proposed to Boss_Hoss that they find a place where they could settle, and this clan was formed.

Initial days

Initially, we tried to get members into the clan. Our good old friend Ankan joined the clan. We regularly posted Clan Notices to attract new players.

Clan Rules

Initially, all members, regardless of Town Hall level, are given the Elder role. This requirement will gradually change as the clan develops.

The Co-leader role is currently reserved for the main accounts of the founding members, Boss_Hoss and Tito, and our good old friend Ankan.
